Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Still have questions?

How does this work?

We connect you with local attorneys using smart technology, and thousands of people have been helped. Complete the short quiz, and if you're eligible you'll be connected to an attorney, often with no upfront fees.

Do I have a case if I do not feel hurt?

Possibly. Adrenaline can mask pain, and symptoms can appear hours or days later. Seek medical care, which also documents your injuries.

Am I obligated to work with you?

No. You have no obligation to this site or to any partner. The attorney offers a free initial consultation, and the quiz takes about 30 seconds.

Do I need a lawyer to get compensated?

It isn't legally required, and you can deal with insurers directly. But most people don't know what a claim is worth, and an attorney can often obtain significantly more. We recommend using the free consultation.

Does Road Traffic Injury charge me anything?

No. The quiz and the initial consultation are free, with no obligation to continue.

Should I take the insurance company's "final offer"?

It may not reflect the true value of your claim, and it may still be negotiable. An attorney can tell you whether it is fair.
