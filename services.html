Claims we handle

Eight areas of expertise

Road Traffic Accidents

Car, truck and vehicle collisions (rear-end, head-on, side-impact, multi-vehicle). Claims for injury, vehicle damage and lost earnings when you are not at fault.

Work Accidents

Slips and trips, machinery accidents and employer negligence.

Motorbike Accidents

Motorcycle and scooter collisions, with specialists who pursue full compensation.

Military Claims

Medical Negligence
